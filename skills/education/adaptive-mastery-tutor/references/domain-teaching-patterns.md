# Domain Teaching Patterns

Select only the pattern relevant to the current subject. Preserve the one-concept mastery loop rather than presenting the whole sequence at once.

## Programming and Software

Progress through concept explanation, reading code or commands, predicting execution, writing a small fragment, finding an error, designing a structure, and applying it to a real project. Inspect the actual codebase before claiming that a file, route, schema, or behavior exists. Test why code behaves as it does, not only whether syntax was memorized.

## Mathematics

Connect intuition to the exact meaning of notation, then use a simple calculation, transformation, application, error analysis, and a novel problem. Evaluate the derivation and assumptions, not only the final number. If arithmetic obscures the concept, simplify the numbers first.

## Language Learning

Move through meaning, structure, contrast with a similar expression, interpretation, original production, realistic conversation, and correction. Evaluate naturalness, register, and context as well as literal correctness. Preserve useful learner intent when rewriting an error.

## Law, Tax, Finance, and Business

Separate actors, rights, duties, facts, assumptions, risks, exceptions, documents, and current rules. Verify current authoritative sources when rules may have changed. Distinguish general education from advice tied to the learner's facts, and identify when professional review is necessary.

## Philosophy and Humanities

Teach the problem that motivates the term, its definition, the argument structure, differences from neighboring views, application, objections, and reconstruction in the learner's own words. Do not reduce understanding to names and quotations.

## Visual Design, Video, and Music

Connect a perceptual analogy to the technical mechanism and the visible or audible result. Use prediction, comparison of strong and weak examples, tool application, and judgment in a real work context. State where a sensory analogy stops matching the mechanism.

## Natural and Social Sciences

Separate observation, model, mechanism, evidence, uncertainty, correlation, and causation. Use predictions and counterfactuals to test the learner's model. Mark simplifications and the scale at which they are valid.

## Practical and Physical Skills

Decompose the action into observable decisions, safety constraints, feedback signals, common failure modes, and increasingly realistic practice. Do not imply that text-only success proves physical competence. Require supervised or qualified instruction where safety demands it.
