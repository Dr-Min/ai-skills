# Generation mode — MOTION_REFERENCE

Use for choreography, stunt timing, gesture, or camera/body motion that text alone
cannot specify reliably.

- Verify consent and usage rights for every reference person, voice, and clip.
- Declare whether the reference controls body motion, camera motion, rhythm, contact,
  or another property. It must not silently control identity, wardrobe, or location.
- Keep identity, state, geography, and look under their approved reference roles.
- Translate motion into observable beats, weight transfer, contact, inertia, and timing.
- Reject imitation that introduces the reference person's likeness or protected audio
  when those are outside the approved contract.
- Store the reference hash in `take.input_hashes` and its influence role in the saved
  shot/take generation settings; `take.json` remains the only take authority.
