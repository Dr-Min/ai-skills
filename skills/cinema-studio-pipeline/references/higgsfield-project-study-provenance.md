## Independently implemented lessons

- Causal story and scene contracts precede visual generation.
- Assets are versioned descriptor-plus-evidence records; states never overwrite one another.
- Every shot is self-contained and binds current positions, references, optics, light, action, acting, and audio.
- Character identity is stress-tested across the actual crops, light, ensembles, and motion the film needs.
- Difficult ensemble scenes use a single continuity ledger plus optional geometry-only control derivatives.
- Long dialogue binds a versioned, hashed, centrally approved `VOICE` asset.
- Rough-cut failures may request bounded missing coverage before fine cut and picture lock.
- An optical carrier plate is only a capability-tested fallback, never a universal model rule.
- Reviewed pixels, media, settings, hashes, and authenticated decisions outrank prompts or successful job messages.

These are generalized engineering and directing rules expressed through this package's own schemas, files, gates, validators, and terminology. Do not present attached project-specific skill names or prompt formulas as components copied into this pipeline.

## Independent modules

`timed-storyboard` and `sync-cuts-to-music` were built as separate local modules. The former owns narrative time, blocking, motion paths, and optional control-map derivatives; the latter analyzes audio transients and proposes edit events. They stay separate until approved events are represented in `timeline.json`.
