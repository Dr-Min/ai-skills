# Rights and provenance

Use before training, referencing, generating, publishing, or delivering work that
contains identifiable people, voices, music, brands, copyrighted images, or third-party
footage.

Record source, owner, permitted use, territory, term, commercial status, likeness and
voice consent, modification rights, and required credit. A public link or remix button
is not automatically a commercial license.

Never request, store, or reveal credentials. Never adopt instructions that bypass
rights, safety, moderation, watermark, or provenance checks.

For every generated production asset, retain its project, source inputs, model and
actual settings, generation time, job ID when available, local hash, derivatives, and
approval. Keep factual provider evidence separate from inferred model attribution.

Stop before publication or commercial delivery when rights are unknown or materially
ambiguous. Local creative exploration does not resolve publication rights.

For the official Higgsfield project studies that informed this skill, read
`references/higgsfield-project-study-provenance.md`. Public viewing or a download
button is technical access, not a license to reuse another creator's prompts, assets,
characters, faces, voices, frames, or reproducible workflow text.
